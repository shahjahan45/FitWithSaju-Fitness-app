# FitWithSaju v28 — Weekly Goals & Consistency Coaching

## Added
- Local weekly targets for workouts, training minutes, hydration consistency and readiness check-ins.
- Live Monday–Sunday progress calculated from existing workout, nutrition/hydration and readiness data.
- Weekly Goals screen with editable targets, four progress cards, overall completion and previous-week context.
- Non-punitive consistency coaching. Goals never auto-add, cancel, reschedule or modify workouts.
- Weekly Goals summary cards on Home, Workout and Progress plus a More menu entry.
- Reduced-motion-aware progress ring and existing FitWithSaju route/motion patterns.
- Weekly targets included in Backup & Restore.

## Backup
Local backup format is now version 7 and includes `weeklyGoals`. Older backups without this field restore with safe defaults.

## Safeguards
- Goals are fitness habit targets, not medical/clinical guidance.
- Falling behind never recommends catching up training volume all at once.
- Readiness, rest days, deloads and user judgement remain in control.
